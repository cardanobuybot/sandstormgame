The Citadel is the one building that decides everything else. Its level sets which buildings and units you have, how many, how high they go, how many builders you own and who you are matched against. This page has the full table and a plan for each level.

## Every Citadel level at a glance

{{table:citadel-unlocks|wide}}

Three rules sit behind this table:

- **A building's top level follows the Citadel.** Something that unlocks at Citadel 3 can be level 1 at Citadel 3, level 2 at Citadel 4, and so on.
- **The upgrade has to fit in your stores.** Citadel 5 costs {{b:citadel.5.cost}} oil; if your tanks hold less, you cannot pay for it however long you save.
- **Each new level pays crystals**, {{n:citadelCrystals}} in total from level 2 to level 8.

## Level by level

### Citadel 1: the first ten minutes

Add the second Pumpjack, a Salvage Yard, a Scrap Depot and your 20 walls, then raid until you have {{b:citadel.2.cost}} oil. Nothing can be upgraded here, so there is no reason to stay. The [beginner's guide](/blog/dune-clash-beginners-guide/) walks through it.

### Citadel 2: research begins

You get the **Ranger**, the **Proving Ground**, two **Mines**, a third Pumpjack, a second Salvage Yard and a second Turret. Build the Proving Ground early and start researching Infantry: it is cheap, and research never needs a builder.

### Citadel 3: vehicles

The **Motor Works** brings the **Ram**, which breaks walls. You also get the **Shellpit**, a second Barracks, a second Oil Tank and a second Scrap Depot. Army space jumps from {{space:2}} to {{space:3}}. This is the first level worth staying at for a while: there are {{limit:wall.3}} walls to place and a real base to design.

### Citadel 4: aircraft, and a third builder

The **Rotor Pad** brings the **Flyer**; the Motor Works adds the **Scavenger**; the **Flak Nest** is your first anti-air gun. The third builder is the quiet star of this level: everything gets a third faster. Build the Flak Nest before anything else, because every opponent just got Flyers too.

### Citadel 5: heavy armour and hidden guns

The **Bulwark** tank, the **Sand Blind**, two **Sky Snares** and a third Barracks. Army space reaches {{space:5}}. From this level the share of your stored oil and scrap that a raid can take drops from 20% to 16%.

### Citadel 6: the long guns

The **Longshot** and the **Wrench Rig** on your side, the **Rocket Battery** on your walls, plus a third Oil Tank and Scrap Depot and a fifth Pumpjack. Upgrades now take most of a day each, so plan what your builders do overnight.

### Citadel 7: the fourth builder

The **Thunderwing** bomber, a fourth Barracks, second Flak Nest and second Sand Blind. Army space passes 200. The loot share drops again, to 14%.

### Citadel 8: the top

No new unit types, but one more of almost everything and the last level for all of it. Army space tops out at {{n:topSpace}}.

## When should you upgrade the Citadel?

There are two schools.

**Upgrade as soon as you can ("rushing").** You reach new units quickly, and each level pays crystals. The price: opponents are chosen by Citadel level, give or take one. A Citadel 6 base with Citadel 3 guns meets Citadel 5, 6 and 7 armies and loses to all of them. You also earn less from weaker bases: raiding a base one Citadel level below yours pays 80% of the loot, two levels below pays half.

**Finish everything first ("maxing").** Your base is as strong as it can be for its level, and raids against you fail more often. The price is time, most of it spent on walls.

A sensible middle is to upgrade when these are done:

1. Every **Oil Tank and Scrap Depot** is at its top level, and the next Citadel fits in your stores.
2. Every **Pumpjack and Salvage Yard** is at its top level.
3. Every **Barracks**, the Motor Works and the Rotor Pad are at their top levels.
4. Every **gun** you are allowed is built, and the newest type is upgraded.
5. The **Proving Ground** is at its top level and your two favourite units are researched.

Walls, mines and the last gun levels can wait: you can finish them after the upgrade, with the bigger income the next level brings.

## Common questions

**Does the Citadel work while it is being upgraded?** Yes. It keeps storing oil and scrap, and in a raid it stands at its current level. One builder is busy until it is done.

**Can I speed it up?** Crystals finish any job at once. The [crystals guide](/blog/dune-clash-crystals-guide/) has the prices; the Citadel is one of the few things worth spending them on.

**Is there a Citadel 9?** Not today. The game has {{n:citadelMax}} levels.
