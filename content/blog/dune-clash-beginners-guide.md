Your first hour in Dune Clash decides nothing for ever, but a good start saves you a day or two of waiting. This guide takes you from the first screen to Citadel 3: what to build, what to skip, and how your first raids go.

## What you start with

A new base has five buildings: a **Citadel**, one **Pumpjack**, one **Oil Tank**, a **Barracks** and a **Turret**. In the bank you have {{n:startOil}} oil, {{n:startScrap}} scrap and {{n:startCrystals}} crystals, and two builders stroll around the base, waiting for work.

{{shot:new-base|Minute one. The helmet at the top shows free builders: 2 of 2.}}

The first time you open the game, Gus, the rig foreman, walks you through one build, one raid and one upgrade. Follow him. It takes a few minutes, and you can leave the tour at any point with "Skip tutorial". His notes stay in the menu (☰) under **How to play**.

## Step 1: build everything Citadel 1 allows

At Citadel 1 nothing can be upgraded yet, but several buildings can be added. Tap **Build**, pick a building, drag it to a free spot and tap **Place**. In this order:

1. **A second Pumpjack** ({{b:collector.1.cost}} oil, {{b:collector.1.time}}). Oil pays for almost everything at the start.
2. **A Salvage Yard** ({{b:salvage.1.cost}} oil). It cuts scrap out of a crashed ship. Scrap is the only thing that pays for guns and walls, and without a Salvage Yard you only get it from raids.
3. **A Scrap Depot** ({{b:depot.1.cost}} oil), so that the scrap has somewhere to go.
4. **Walls**: you may place {{limit:wall.1}}, at {{b:wall.1.cost}} scrap each. Put them between the edge of the map and your Oil Tank and Citadel for now. You will rearrange them many times.

Buildings at this stage finish in ten seconds, so both builders are free again almost at once.

## Step 2: your first raid

Tap **Attack**. The game finds a base near your level and gives you {{n:scoutSeconds}} seconds to look at it. Your Barracks holds 20 army space, which at this point means 20 Infantry.

- Pick **Infantry** in the bar at the bottom, then tap the sand **outside the red border**. Tap for one soldier, hold for a stream.
- Land them near the buildings that hold loot: Pumpjacks, Oil Tanks, the Salvage Yard, the Citadel.
- Look at the **Tremors** chip while you scout. If it says "high", the Sandmaw is coming. Do not drop everyone on one spot.

Your troops cost nothing, win or lose. Whatever the raid brings home is profit, so raid often. The [attack guide](/blog/how-to-get-three-stars-attack-guide/) covers this properly.

## Step 3: upgrade the Citadel to level 2

Citadel 2 costs {{b:citadel.2.cost}} oil and takes {{b:citadel.2.time}}. It is the most important thing you can buy in the first hour, because every other building is capped by the Citadel: at Citadel 1 everything stays at level 1.

You will not have {{b:citadel.2.cost}} oil straight away. Raid a few times, tap your Pumpjacks to collect (or use the **Collect all** button), and you are there. Reaching Citadel 2 also pays 10 crystals.

While the Citadel is upgrading, one builder is busy. Use the other.

## Step 4: what Citadel 2 opens

- **Ranger**, your second unit: rocket troopers that hit defenses from a distance.
- **Proving Ground**, where units are researched to higher levels.
- **Mines**: two of them, buried and invisible to raiders.
- A third Pumpjack, a second Salvage Yard, a second Turret and 30 more walls.
- Level 2 for everything you already have.

A sensible order: third Pumpjack and second Salvage Yard first (income), then the second Turret and walls (so the income stays yours), then the Proving Ground, then level 2 upgrades wherever a green arrow shows.

{{shot:upgrade|Tap a building, then Upgrade. The green line shows what the next level gives.}}

## Step 5: aim for Citadel 3

Citadel 3 costs {{b:citadel.3.cost}} oil and takes {{b:citadel.3.time}}. It brings the **Motor Works** and your first vehicle, the wall-breaking **Ram**, plus the **Shellpit**, a second Oil Tank, a second Scrap Depot and a second Barracks. With the second Barracks and the Motor Works, your army goes from {{space:2}} to {{space:3}} places.

Before you start it, get your Oil Tank to level 2: the Citadel and one level 1 tank cannot even hold {{b:citadel.3.cost}} oil. The table of [what every Citadel level unlocks](/blog/citadel-upgrade-guide/) shows what comes after.

## Habits that pay off from day one

- **Keep both builders busy.** An idle builder is the most expensive thing in the game. Before you close the app, start the longest job you can afford.
- **Empty your Pumpjacks and Salvage Yards.** A level 1 Pumpjack is full after six hours and then stops. Raiders also take half of whatever sits inside.
- **Spend before you log off.** Oil in a tank can be stolen. Oil turned into an upgrade cannot.
- **Do not hoard crystals for small things.** Finishing a ten-minute job costs a few crystals you will want later. See the [crystals guide](/blog/dune-clash-crystals-guide/).
- **Watch the replay when someone raids you.** The **Log** button shows every attack on your base. One replay teaches more about your layout than an hour of guessing.

## One thing to do today: save your base

Your base lives in a guest account tied to the phone or browser you are playing on. Lose the phone or clear the browser, and the base is gone. On iPhone, open ☰ and tap **Save progress with Game Center**. In a browser, or to move to another phone, use a transfer code. Both take a minute: [how to save and move your base](/blog/save-and-transfer-your-base/).
