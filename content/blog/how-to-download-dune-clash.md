## iPhone

1. Open the **[Dune Clash page on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/dune-clash/id6837478004)**, or search the App Store for "Dune Clash".
2. Tap **Get**.
3. Open the game. It starts at once: no account, no e-mail address, no password.

The download is small, well under 20 MB. The game needs iOS 15.0 or later and is made for iPhone.

**How to recognise the right app.** The name is "Dune Clash", the icon shows a brown sand worm roaring in front of an orange sun, and the link above goes straight to it. It was called "Dune of Clash" during testing, so you may still see that name in older posts.

### If the App Store says the page cannot be found

At the moment the game is **not listed in the App Store of European Union countries**. EU law asks app developers to have their trader details verified before an app can be offered there. Ours were sent in before the release and Apple is still checking them. Nothing is wrong with your phone or your account.

Until that is finished, players in the EU can use the browser version below. It is the same game on the same server, and when the App Store listing opens you can move your base into the app with a transfer code.

## Android

The Android version is in **closed testing** on Google Play. That is a step Google requires of new developers before a public release. It is not open to everyone yet.

When it opens, the button on [our Dune Clash page](/dune-clash/) will turn into a Google Play link.

**Please do not install "Dune Clash" from anywhere else.** We do not publish APK files, and we have not given the game to any other store or download site. A file offered under our name outside Google Play did not come from us.

In the meantime, the browser version runs well in Chrome on Android.

## Web browser

Open **[dune-of-clash.vercel.app](https://dune-of-clash.vercel.app/)** on a phone, tablet or computer. That is all: the game loads and you are playing.

It is the full game, with the same bases, raids and players. Two things differ: there is no shop, and your base is remembered by that browser, so read the note on saving below. More in [playing Dune Clash in your browser](/blog/play-dune-clash-in-browser/).

## What it costs

Nothing. The game is free on every platform and has no adverts.

The iPhone and Android apps sell optional packs of crystals, from US $0.99 to US $49.99, shown in your local currency. Crystals shorten waiting times; they do not unlock anything a free player cannot get. The browser version sells nothing. Details in the [crystals guide](/blog/dune-clash-crystals-guide/).

## What you need

- **An internet connection.** Dune Clash is an online game: your base lives on the server, and other players raid it.
- **English.** The game is in English only for now.
- **A minute.** There is no sign-up of any kind.

## Before you play for long: save your base

The game makes a guest account for you on the device you start on. That is convenient, and it has one catch: until you save it, **the base exists only on that phone or in that browser**.

- On iPhone: menu ☰, **Save progress with Game Center**.
- On Android: menu ☰, **Save progress with Google**.
- In a browser: move the base to a phone with ☰, **Show code**.

It takes a minute. The steps are in [how to save and move your base](/blog/save-and-transfer-your-base/).

## Age rating

Dune Clash is rated 9+ on the App Store. It has cartoon battles between soldiers, tanks and a giant worm, no blood, no chat and no adverts. We make it for players of 13 and up. Parents can find the full picture in [is Dune Clash safe for kids?](/blog/is-dune-clash-safe-for-kids-parents-guide/)

## Trouble?

- **The game does not load.** Check your connection and start it again.
- **Crystals did not arrive after a purchase.** Keep the game open with a connection, or restart it: an unfinished purchase is credited at the next start, and never twice.
- **Anything else.** The [support page](https://dune-of-clash.vercel.app/support.html) has the common answers and our e-mail address.
