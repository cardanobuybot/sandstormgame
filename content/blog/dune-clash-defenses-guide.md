You are not there when your base is attacked. Your defenses fight alone, by fixed rules, and the raider has {{n:scoutSeconds}} seconds to study them first. This guide explains those rules, so that you can build a base that works without you.

## The five guns at a glance

{{table:defenses|wide}}

All defenses, walls and traps are paid with **scrap**, not oil. If you are short of scrap, see the [economy guide](/blog/oil-and-scrap-economy-guide/).

## How a gun picks its target

Every gun follows the same three rules:

1. **It shoots the nearest enemy it is allowed to hit.** Not the strongest, not the most dangerous: the nearest. A Bulwark driving in front of a group of Rangers soaks up the fire meant for them.
2. **It only sees its own kind of target.** Turrets and Shellpits shoot ground units only. Flak Nests shoot aircraft only. Sand Blinds and Rocket Batteries shoot both.
3. **Range is measured from the gun.** Anything outside the circle is ignored, even if it is destroying the building next door.

Two things follow. A gun on the edge of the base wastes half its circle on empty sand. And a gun with nothing to shoot at is dead weight: a base with four Turrets and no Flak Nest cannot touch a single Flyer.

## Turret

Fast and cheap, available from the first minute. It deals extra damage (×1.5) to light units: Infantry, Rangers and Scavengers. Against a Bulwark it is a pea-shooter.

**Place it** where foot soldiers arrive: covering your Pumpjacks, tanks and the approaches to the Citadel. Turrets work best in pairs whose circles overlap.

**Its weakness** is reach. Longshots fire from 9 cells, and no Turret reaches further than 7.5.

## Shellpit

A mortar with the longest reach of the early guns. Each shell lands on a spot and damages everything around it, so a crowd of Infantry can vanish in two shots.

It has two blind spots. It cannot hit anything closer than **three cells**, and its shells take 1.2 seconds to land on the place where the target *was*. Fast units, such as Scavengers, simply drive out from under them.

**Place it** in the middle of the base, behind walls, with a Turret close enough to cover the ring it cannot reach.

{{levels:shellpit}}

## Flak Nest

The anti-aircraft gun. It hits hard, reaches far and ignores everything on the ground. A level 1 Flak Nest brings down a level 1 Flyer in two shots.

**Place it** near the centre, so that its circle covers the Citadel and your stores. Raiders love to find a Flak Nest on the edge: they kill it with ground troops, then send in the aircraft.

{{levels:flak}}

## Sand Blind

A gun the raider cannot see. It stays under the sand until an enemy comes within 4.5 cells, then rises and opens fire a moment later. It shoots twice a second at ground units and aircraft alike, and deals ×1.5 damage to armoured vehicles.

**Place it** where vehicles will be led: behind the wall a Ram is likely to break, or beside a tempting Oil Tank. Its reach is short, so a Sand Blind far from anything worth attacking never fires.

{{levels:blind}}

## Rocket Battery

The top defense: 10 cells of reach, ground and air, and ×1.5 damage to armour. One rocket takes about half of a Longshot's health. It reloads slowly, though, so swarms of cheap troops overwhelm it.

**Place it** deep in the base, where it can reach every side, with Turrets nearby to handle the crowd.

{{levels:rocket}}

## Walls, mines and snares

- **Walls** buy time. Every second a unit spends chewing a wall is a second your guns shoot it for free. See the [walls guide](/blog/walls-guide/).
- **Mines** are invisible and go off under ground units. **Sky Snares** do the same to aircraft. Both are covered in [hidden defenses](/blog/traps-guide-mines-sand-blinds-sky-snares/).

## What every raider is afraid of

Think about your base from the other side.

| If the raider brings | They fear | So build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crowds of Infantry and Rangers | Area damage | Shellpits, Mines, Turrets with overlapping fire |
| Bulwarks and Rams | Guns with a bonus against armour | Sand Blinds, Rocket Batteries, thick walls |
| Longshots (range 9) | Anything that can reach them | Rocket Batteries, Shellpits (range 10) |
| Flyers and Thunderwings | Anti-air | Flak Nests, Sky Snares, Sand Blinds, Rocket Batteries |
| Scavengers after your stores | Being shot before they arrive | Turrets beside tanks and depots |

A good base has an answer in every row.

## Which defense to upgrade first

- **Citadel 1 and 2:** the second Turret, both Mines, then walls.
- **Citadel 3:** the Shellpit as soon as you can afford it. It changes how raiders must attack.
- **Citadel 4:** the Flak Nest first. Flyers appear at this level, and a base without anti-air gives away three stars.
- **Citadel 5:** the Sand Blind and both Sky Snares. Bulwarks arrive now; the Sand Blind is your answer.
- **Citadel 6 and later:** the Rocket Battery, then the second Shellpit, then levels for everything.

Within one type, a new gun is usually worth more than a level on an old one: it adds a whole new circle of fire.

## Three facts that surprise new players

1. **Nothing needs repairs.** After a raid every building, wall and mine is back. Defending costs you nothing but the loot.
2. **A gun that is being upgraded still fights**, at its current level. Only a gun that is being built for the first time is weak: it counts as level 1.
3. **Guns cannot see the Sandmaw**, and it does not hurt buildings. The worm is the raider's problem. It is, in a way, your free extra defense.

To see how your own base really performs, open the **Log** and watch a replay of the last raid. Then read the [base layout guide](/blog/dune-clash-base-layout-guide/).
