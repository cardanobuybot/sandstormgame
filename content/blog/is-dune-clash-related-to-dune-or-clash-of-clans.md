Short answer: **no**. Dune Clash is an original game made by Sandstorm Game, an independent one-person studio. It is not part of the Dune franchise, it is not licensed by its owners, and it is not made by Supercell, the company behind Clash of Clans. Nobody from those companies had anything to do with it.

The name is two ordinary English words, and they describe the game: a **dune** is a hill of sand, and a **clash** is a fight. It is a game about fighting in the sand.

The rest of this page explains why people ask, what is and is not similar, and where to go if you were actually looking for one of the other two.

## What Dune Clash is

A free build-and-raid strategy game for iPhone and web browsers, with Android in testing. You build a base on a desert planet, pump oil, cut crashed spaceships into scrap, and raid other players in three-minute battles while a giant worm called the Sandmaw hunts your ground troops. [The full description is here](/blog/what-is-dune-clash/).

It came out on the App Store on 24 November 2026. Before 18 November 2026 it was called "Dune of Clash" during testing, which is why the web version still lives at an address with that name in it.

## Is it connected to Dune?

No. *Dune* is a 1965 science-fiction novel by Frank Herbert, followed by more novels, several films (the most recent series directed by Denis Villeneuve, with *Dune: Part Three* reaching cinemas in December 2026), a television series and many licensed games. That universe has its own planet, its own houses, its own history and its own owners.

None of that is in Dune Clash:

- **No shared story, characters or places.** The planet in Dune Clash has no name. There are no noble houses, no desert tribes, no emperor.
- **A different economy.** The famous resource of the novels does not exist here. Dune Clash runs on oil and on scrap metal from wrecked ships.
- **A different worm.** The Sandmaw is our own creature, designed for this game, with its own look and its own rules: it is a neutral monster that turns up in about half of all raids and can be shot dead.

What the two do share is a setting that is older than both: a desert, and something enormous living under it. Giant worms in the sand have been turning up in folklore, novels, films and games for a very long time. We wrote about that separately: [giant sand worms in fiction and games](/blog/sand-worms-in-games-and-fiction/).

## Is it connected to Clash of Clans?

No. Clash of Clans is a mobile game released in 2012 by the Finnish studio Supercell. It made the build-and-raid formula famous: grow a village, train an army, attack other players' villages for loot and stars.

Dune Clash belongs to the same genre, the way two football games are both football games. It is made by a different studio, with different art, different units and different rules. Some of the differences are large:

| | Clash of Clans | Dune Clash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Made by | Supercell (Finland) | Sandstorm Game (independent) |
| First released | 2012 | November 2026 |
| Setting | Fantasy village | Desert planet with oil rigs and wrecked spaceships |
| Clans | Yes, with clan wars | Not yet (planned) |
| A neutral monster during raids | No | Yes, the Sandmaw |
| Size | One of the biggest mobile games in the world | Brand new and very small |

A longer and fairer comparison is in [games like Clash of Clans: how Dune Clash compares](/blog/games-like-clash-of-clans-dune-clash/).

## Why the name, then?

Because it says what the game is in two words. The base stands among dunes, and the game is about raids. Plenty of games have "clash" in their names and plenty are set among dunes; both are common words, and neither belongs to one company when it is used in its plain meaning.

We do understand that the combination makes people think of two famous things at once. That is why this page exists, and why every page of this site carries the same line at the bottom: Dune Clash is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Dune novels and films or their owners, or to Supercell and Clash of Clans.

## If you were looking for an official Dune game

They exist, made by other companies under licence. Search for *Dune: Awakening* (a survival game) or *Dune: Spice Wars* (a strategy game for computers), both published by Funcom, or the digital edition of the board game *Dune: Imperium*. The game that started real-time strategy as we know it, *Dune II*, came out in 1992; its story is part of our [short history of build-and-raid games](/blog/from-dune-ii-to-mobile-history-of-base-building-games/).

## If you were looking for Clash of Clans

It is in the App Store and on Google Play under the name "Clash of Clans", published by Supercell.
