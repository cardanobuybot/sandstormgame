Aircraft change the game twice: once at Citadel 4, when the Flyer arrives, and again at Citadel 7 with the Thunderwing. They ignore most of what a base is built from. They also die faster than anything else when the defender is ready. This guide covers both halves.

## What aircraft ignore

- **Walls.** They fly over.
- **Mines.** Only ground units set them off.
- **Turrets and Shellpits.** Neither can aim upwards. Before Citadel 4 a base has nothing that can touch an aircraft.
- **The Sandmaw.** The worm hunts ground units only. An army made entirely of aircraft gives it nothing to eat, and it never even surfaces.

That last point matters more than it looks. When the scouting screen says **Tremors: high**, flying is the simplest answer there is. Details in [how to beat the Sandmaw](/blog/how-to-beat-the-sandmaw/).

## What shoots them down

{{table:anti-air|wide}}

The **Flak Nest** is the main threat: long reach, one shot a second, and two shots for a Flyer of the same tier. The **Sky Snare** is the nasty surprise: invisible, and one burst kills every Flyer near it. Sand Blinds and Rocket Batteries shoot at anything, aircraft included.

## The Flyer

{{unit:flyer}}

A gyrocopter. It heads for defenses before anything else, and it is quick: 2.4 cells a second, faster than Infantry.

{{unit-levels:flyer}}

A Flyer takes 5 army space, so a full Citadel 4 army ({{space:4}} space) is 15 of them.

**Flyers are good at:**

- Bases with **no Flak Nest**, or with one near the edge that ground troops can kill first.
- Killing **Shellpits and Turrets**, which cannot shoot back.
- Raids with **high tremors**.

**Flyers are bad at:**

- Flying past a living Flak Nest. Six Flyers arriving together lose one every two seconds.
- Anything that needs time. Their damage is modest, and they chase guns all over the map while the clock runs.

## The Thunderwing

{{unit:thunderwing}}

A heavy bomber: slow, five times a Flyer's health, and every bomb damages all buildings within 1.6 cells of where it lands. Two Oil Tanks side by side are one target to a Thunderwing.

{{unit-levels:thunderwing}}

At 12 army space it is the most expensive unit in the game. A full Citadel 7 army ({{space:7}} space) holds 16 of them.

**Thunderwings are good at:**

- **Tightly packed bases.** The closer the buildings, the more each bomb hits.
- **Waking hidden guns.** A Sand Blind caught in a blast is revealed.
- **Outlasting anti-air.** It takes a level 1 Flak Nest ten shots to bring one down.

**Thunderwings are bad at:**

- **Speed.** At 1.3 cells a second it crosses the base slowly, and it only attacks every two and a half seconds.
- **Sky Snares.** Three snares finish one, and a bomber formation tends to fly over all of them in a row.

## Planning an air raid

**1. Count the anti-air while you scout.** How many Flak Nests, and where? From Citadel 5, assume Sky Snares on the straight line between the edge and the nearest gun. From Citadel 6, find the Rocket Battery.

**2. Decide who kills the Flak.** Three ways:

- *Ground first.* A Bulwark with Rangers behind it, landed on the side nearest the Flak Nest. Flak ignores them completely.
- *Longshots.* They shell a Flak Nest from 9 cells away, beyond the reach of every Turret, and the Flak Nest cannot shoot at the ground.
- *Brute force.* Enough Thunderwings to trade. Expensive, but it works when the Flak Nests are few and low.

**3. Send a scout.** One Flyer ahead of the rest finds the first Sky Snare for the price of 5 army space.

**4. Spread the landing.** Aircraft dropped along a line reach the target one after another and trigger snares one at a time. Aircraft dropped on one spot share one burst.

**5. Come from the side the guns are on.** Aircraft go for defenses first. Start them next to the defenses that matter and they clear your path for the ground troops; start them on the far side and they fly over the whole base to get there.

## Mixed armies

Aircraft and ground troops cover each other's weaknesses, because almost every defense can hit only one of them.

- **Bulwarks in front, Flyers behind.** Turrets and Shellpits shoot the tanks; the Flyers kill the Turrets and Shellpits.
- **Rams and Infantry to open the Flak, Thunderwings to finish.** The classic two-wave raid.
- **A few Flyers in a ground army** as insurance against the worm: when the Sandmaw surfaces, nearby aircraft turn on it too, and it cannot bite them.

## Defending against aircraft

From the other side, the checklist is short:

1. **Build the Flak Nest the hour you reach Citadel 4**, and keep it at the top level your Citadel allows. Nothing else you own shoots up until Citadel 5.
2. **Put it in the middle**, behind walls, inside the range of Turrets that protect it from ground troops.
3. **Place Sky Snares on the approach to your guns**, not in the corners.
4. **Spread your buildings a little** once Citadel 7 opponents appear. Leave one empty cell between your stores, and a bomb aimed at one no longer reaches the next.
