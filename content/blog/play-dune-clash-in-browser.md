You can play Dune Clash without installing anything. Open one address in a web browser and the game starts: the same game, on the same server, against the same players as the iPhone app.

**The address: [dune-of-clash.vercel.app](https://dune-of-clash.vercel.app/)**

## How to start

1. Open the address above in a browser on your phone, tablet or computer.
2. Wait a few seconds for the game to load.
3. Play. Gus, the rig foreman, walks you through your first building and your first raid.

There is no sign-up, no e-mail address and no password. The game creates a guest account for you the moment it opens.

## Who the browser version is for

- **Anyone who wants to try before installing.** Twenty minutes in a tab will tell you whether you like the game.
- **Android players**, while the Android app is still in closed testing.
- **Players in the European Union**, where the game is not in the App Store yet.
- **People at a computer.** A mouse works where a finger would: click to select, click and hold to land a stream of troops, drag to move around the map.

## What is the same

Everything that matters:

- **The whole game**: all {{n:buildingCount}} buildings, all {{n:unitCount}} units, every Citadel level.
- **The same world.** You are matched with players on iPhone and Android, and they with you. Trophies and the Ladder are shared.
- **The same rules**, checked by the same server.
- **Sound and music**, with volume sliders in the menu.

## Keeping your base

In the browser, the key to your base is stored in that browser, on that device. The base itself is safe on our server, but only that browser knows which base is yours.

You will lose access to it if you:

- clear the browser's history and site data,
- play in a **private or incognito window** (it forgets everything when you close it),
- switch to a different browser or a different device,
- reset or lose the device.

There are two ways to stay safe.

### Move the base into the app

If you have, or later get, the iPhone or Android app:

1. In the browser, open the menu ☰ and tap **Show code**. A code of twelve characters appears. It works once, for 15 minutes.
2. In the app, open ☰ and tap **Enter code**. Type the code.
3. The app shows which base the code belongs to. Confirm.
4. In the app, tap **Save progress with Game Center** (or **Google**). Now the base is tied to your account for good.

The base stays open in the browser too. You can keep playing in both.

### Or keep a second copy alive

No app? Open the game in a second browser, or on a second device, and move the base there with a code in the same way. Two places know your base now, and losing one is no longer the end of it.

Full steps, with what each screen says, are in [how to save and move your base](/blog/save-and-transfer-your-base/).

**Never give a transfer code to anyone.** Whoever types it gets your base.

## Tips for playing in a browser

- **Use an up-to-date browser.** Current versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox all work.
- **Bookmark the address**, so you always come back to the right one.
- **Stick to one browser.** A base started in Chrome is not visible from Safari unless you move it with a code.
- **Keep the tab in front during a raid.** A battle has a time limit, and a raid you walk away from is a raid lost.
- **Hold to deploy.** On a touchscreen, hold a finger on the sand outside the red border to land a stream of troops. With a mouse, hold the button.

## Is it safe?

The browser version asks for no personal details and no permissions: no location, no camera, no notifications. It has no adverts and no tracking. What the game stores about you is listed in the short [privacy policy](https://dune-of-clash.vercel.app/privacy.html), and you can erase your account at any time from the menu with **Delete account**.

## Common questions

**Does it cost anything?** No. There is nothing to buy in the browser version.

**Will my progress carry over to the app?** Yes, with a transfer code. See above.

**Why is the address not on sandstormgame.xyz?** The game runs on its own address, which still carries its working title, "Dune of Clash". This site, sandstormgame.xyz, is the studio's home page and the place for guides.

**Can I play offline?** No. Your base lives on the server and other players raid it, so the game needs a connection.
