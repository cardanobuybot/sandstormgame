In Dune Clash units cost nothing and need no training. Before every raid you choose any mix of the units you have unlocked, up to your army space. That makes experimenting free, and it means there is no reason to bring the same army to every base.

These five are starting points. Each is built from how the units actually behave, with counts that fill the army space of a fully built base at that Citadel level. If your Barracks are a level behind, drop a few Infantry.

## 1. The Tide (Citadel 1 and 2)

**The mix at Citadel 2:** 12 Infantry and 6 Rangers ({{space:2}} space).

At Citadel 1 it is simply 20 Infantry. From Citadel 2, a third of the space goes to Rangers: they shoot from 3.5 cells away and deal ×1.5 damage to defenses.

**How to land it.** Infantry first, in a line along the edge nearest the Turret. Rangers two seconds later, behind them. The Turret shoots the nearest target, which is now an Infantry soldier, while the Rangers take the Turret apart.

**Avoid** bases where two Turrets cover the same ground and a Mine sits in the only gap. At this level you cannot break walls quickly; go around or pick another base.

## 2. Ram and Rush (Citadel 3)

**The mix:** 3 Rams, 30 Infantry and 10 Rangers ({{space:3}} space).

Citadel 3 is where bases get a real wall and a Shellpit. The Rams answer the wall: twelve times their damage against it, and they drive straight at it instead of looking for a way round.

**How to land it.** Rams first, pointed at the stretch of wall closest to the Citadel. Infantry right behind, **spread wide**: the Shellpit's shells hit an area, and a tight crowd is exactly what it wants. Rangers last. Once the wall is open, everything on the ground turns and goes through the hole.

**Avoid** bases with the Shellpit dead centre and both Turrets beside it, unless the tremors are low. A worm on top of a Shellpit is too much for thirty Infantry.

## 3. Sky Patrol (Citadel 4)

**The mix:** 12 Flyers and 18 Infantry ({{space:4}} space).

Flyers ignore walls, Mines, Turrets and Shellpits. The only thing at this level that can touch them is the Flak Nest. A Citadel 3 base cannot have one at all, and a Citadel 4 base has at most one.

**How to land it.** If there is a Flak Nest, land the Infantry on its side first: Flak cannot shoot at the ground. Once it is down, land the Flyers next to the remaining guns. They hunt defenses first, and nothing left can shoot back.

**This is also the worm-proof army.** With "Tremors: high", hold the Infantry until the Flyers have cleared the guns, or leave them out entirely.

**Avoid** bases with a high-level Flak Nest in the middle, behind walls. Bring Ram and Rush instead.

## 4. Steel Column (Citadel 5 and 6)

**The mix at Citadel 5:** 4 Bulwarks, 3 Rams, 20 Rangers and 34 Infantry ({{space:5}} space).

**The mix at Citadel 6:** 4 Bulwarks, 2 Wrench Rigs, 6 Longshots, 3 Rams and 15 Rangers ({{space:6}} space).

The Bulwark has 1,500 health at level 1 and goes for defenses first. Four of them walking in a row are a moving wall that every Turret and Shellpit wastes its fire on. At Citadel 6, Wrench Rigs repair the tanks as they go, and Longshots flatten guns from 9 cells away, beyond the reach of any Turret.

**How to land it.** Bulwarks first, a couple of cells apart. Rams beside them. Wrench Rigs directly behind the Bulwarks: they follow vehicles and repair the most damaged one. Longshots and Rangers last and furthest back.

**Avoid** bases with a Rocket Battery in reach of your landing side and no way to rush it: rockets deal ×1.5 to armour. Sand Blinds do the same, and you will not see them. Keep a dozen Infantry in reserve to swarm whatever pops up.

**The worm and this army.** Vehicles are slow and the Sandmaw is not. With high tremors, keep the column together so that it can shoot the worm when it surfaces, and never send the Wrench Rigs or Longshots off on their own.

## 5. Thunder Run (Citadel 7 and 8)

**The mix at Citadel 7:** 10 Thunderwings, 4 Bulwarks, 3 Rams and 15 Rangers ({{space:7}} space).

The Thunderwing's bombs damage every building within 1.6 cells of where they fall, and at this level bases are packed tight. Ten bombers erase the middle of a base. They are slow, though, and Flak Nests, Sky Snares and Rocket Batteries all want them.

**How to land it.** Two waves. The ground half goes first, on the side with the most anti-air: Bulwarks in front, Rams at the wall, Rangers behind. Their job is the Flak Nests. When the flak is down, or busy, land the Thunderwings in a line along another side, so that one Sky Snare cannot catch them all.

**Avoid** bases with two Flak Nests far apart and deep inside. You will kill one. Bring an all-ground Steel Column with more Longshots instead.

## A bonus: the Loot Run

**The mix at Citadel 4:** 16 Scavengers, 2 Rams and 22 Infantry ({{space:4}} space).

Not for stars. The Scavenger is the fastest unit in the game, heads straight for oil and scrap buildings, and deals double damage to them. Loot leaves a building as it takes damage, so you do not even need to finish the tanks off.

Land the Rams at the wall nearest the stores, the Infantry as a distraction on the *other* side, then the Scavengers behind the Rams. Take what you came for and tap **End battle**.

## How to choose between them

Ask three questions while you scout:

1. **Are the tremors high?** Then favour aircraft, or keep the army together.
2. **What anti-air is there, and where?** None or weak: fly. Strong and central: stay on the ground.
3. **What do I want?** Stars need the whole base. Loot needs only the stores.
