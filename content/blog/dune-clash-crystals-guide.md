Crystals are the one thing in Dune Clash you can buy with real money. They are also handed out for free, and you never need to buy any. This page gives the exact prices, so you can decide for yourself what they are worth.

## What crystals do

Two things, and nothing else:

1. **Finish a job at once**: any building, upgrade or research that is still running.
2. **Buy the oil or scrap you are short of** for one purchase. If you tap Upgrade without enough oil, the game offers the missing amount for crystals.

Crystals do not buy trophies, units, extra builders or anything a patient player cannot get. They buy time.

## What finishing a job costs

The price depends only on the time left, not on what is being built.

{{table:crystal-finish}}

Between those points the price rises evenly and is rounded up. The longest job in the game, three days, costs 500 crystals from the start, and less with every hour that passes.

Look at what one crystal buys:

- On a 10-minute job, one crystal buys two and a half minutes.
- On a 1-hour job, three minutes.
- On a 1-day job, almost six minutes.
- On a 3-day job, more than eight and a half minutes.

**Crystals go furthest on long jobs.** Spending them on short timers is the worst deal in the game, and it is exactly where new players spend them.

## What oil and scrap cost

{{table:crystal-buy}}

Small amounts are expensive: at 1,000 oil for 4 crystals, one crystal buys 250 oil. Large amounts are cheap: at a million for 500 crystals, one crystal buys 2,000. The purchase has to fit in your stores, so you cannot buy your way past a storage limit.

Compare that with raiding. At Citadel 5 one good raid brings home around 20,000 oil in three and a half minutes, for free. The same oil costs 29 crystals.

## The packs

{{table:crystal-packs}}

Packs are sold only inside the iPhone app, through the App Store, and inside the Android app, through Google Play. The store shows the price in your own currency before you pay. **The browser version has no shop at all**: there, crystals come only from playing.

Purchases are final apart from the store's own refund rules, and crystals cannot be turned back into money or moved to another account. The full rules are in the game's [Terms of Use](https://dune-of-clash.vercel.app/terms.html).

## What is worth spending crystals on

**Good uses:**

- **The last stretch of a long upgrade** when it blocks everything else: the Citadel, or the Proving Ground before a research you are waiting for.
- **A long job you want finished before you go away** for a few days, so your builders are not stuck on it.
- **The missing few thousand** for a Citadel upgrade when your stores are nearly full and a raid could take the lot. Buying 5,000 oil costs 12 crystals; losing 20,000 to a raider costs more.

**Poor uses:**

- **Short timers.** Anything under an hour will be done by the time you finish two raids.
- **Buying oil in bulk** when you could raid for it.
- **Finishing a job the moment it starts.** The price falls every minute. If you are going to pay, wait as long as you can stand.

## A simple plan for free crystals

1. Keep the first {{n:startCrystals}} until at least Citadel 3. Nothing before that takes long enough to deserve them.
2. Win one raid every day you play. Five crystals is small; 150 a month is a three-day upgrade, finished at once, every three to four months, for nothing.
3. Use Citadel rewards on the next Citadel: the 50 crystals for reaching Citadel 5 will finish the last four hours of the day-long upgrade to Citadel 6.

More ways to progress without paying are in the [free-to-play guide](/blog/free-to-play-guide/).

## For parents

Crystals are an in-app purchase. The game never shows adverts and never asks for card details itself: every payment goes through Apple or Google, with whatever purchase controls you have set on the device. How to switch purchases off, and what else the game does and does not do, is in our [guide for parents](/blog/is-dune-clash-safe-for-kids-parents-guide/).
