Dune Clash is free, has no adverts, and sells exactly one thing: packs of crystals that make timers shorter. Everything else is earned by playing. This guide is about doing that well, with the real numbers for how long it takes.

## What money can and cannot do

Crystals **can** finish a building, upgrade or research at once, and buy oil or scrap you are short of.

Crystals **cannot** buy a unit, a building, a builder, a trophy or a shield. Nothing in the game is locked behind payment. There is no premium unit, no subscription and no battle pass.

So a paying player and a free player end up with the same base and the same army. One gets there sooner.

## What you get for free

- **Every unit, already trained.** Units cost nothing and take no time. You can raid as often as you have minutes for.
- **{{n:freeCrystals}} crystals** from your starting stock and Citadel rewards, plus {{n:dailyCrystals}} for the first won raid of every day.
- **Up to four builders.** They come with Citadel levels 4 and 7. They are not sold.
- **Raids on Echoes and Outposts** whenever no real opponent is free, so you are never out of targets.

## Habit 1: never leave a builder idle

This is the whole game in one sentence. Timers only matter if something is running on them.

- Before you close the game, **every builder gets a job**.
- Give the **longest jobs to the night**. An eight-hour upgrade started at bedtime costs you nothing; started at breakfast, it blocks a builder all day.
- Keep **short jobs for when you are playing**: you can refill the builder as soon as it is free.
- If you cannot afford anything, raid until you can. Do not close the game with a free builder.

## Habit 2: keep the Proving Ground busy

Research needs no builder, only oil. It is a fifth queue that many players forget.

Research the units you actually use, cheapest first. Infantry and Rangers stay useful at every Citadel level, and all five of their upgrades together cost less than one late Bulwark level.

## Habit 3: raid for income, pump for background

Your Pumpjacks are steady. Raids are fast.

{{table:echo-loot}}

One clean raid is worth roughly half an hour to an hour of your own production, and takes three and a half minutes. Ten minutes of raiding before you spend is the difference between affording the next upgrade and not.

Raid with a purpose:

- **Short of oil?** Look for full Oil Tanks and bring Scavengers.
- **Short of scrap?** The same, for Scrap Depots.
- **Stores nearly full?** Stop. Loot that does not fit is lost.

## Habit 4: spend to zero before you leave

Whatever sits in your stores can be raided. Whatever has been spent cannot.

1. Start the biggest upgrades you can afford.
2. Queue a research.
3. Put leftover scrap into walls: they are instant and need no builder.
4. **Collect all**, so the pumps start filling from empty.

## Habit 5: collect every six hours if you can

Pumpjacks and Salvage Yards stop when full, and at every level that takes {{b:collector.1.fullAfter}}. Morning, afternoon, evening and once before bed keeps them running all day. Twice a day gets you half.

## Habit 6: win once a day

The first raid of the day with at least one star pays {{n:dailyCrystals}} crystals. One star is half the buildings or the Citadel, and an Outpost counts. It is the cheapest reward in the game.

## Habit 7: spend crystals on long timers only

A crystal buys two and a half minutes of a ten-minute job and more than eight minutes of a three-day job. Save them for the Citadel and for the late, day-long upgrades. The sums are in the [crystals guide](/blog/dune-clash-crystals-guide/).

## Habit 8: take every revenge

A revenge raid holds at least as much oil as you lost, costs the other player nothing and carries no risk for you. You have {{n:revengeHours}} hours. See [trophies, shields and revenge](/blog/trophies-ladder-shields-explained/).

## Habit 9: do not rush past your defenses

A higher Citadel means stronger opponents at once and a smaller loot share from weaker bases. Bring stores, income, army buildings and your main guns up to date before each Citadel upgrade. The checklist is in the [Citadel guide](/blog/citadel-upgrade-guide/).

## Habit 10: save your base

None of this matters if the base is lost with the phone. Saving takes a minute and costs nothing: [how to save and move your base](/blog/save-and-transfer-your-base/).

## An honest word about paying

We make the game, and crystal packs are how it pays for its server. If you enjoy it and want to support it, a pack is the way. But the game is built so that you never have to, and nothing on this page gets easier if you do. It only gets quicker.
