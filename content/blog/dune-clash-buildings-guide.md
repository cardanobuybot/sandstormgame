Dune Clash has {{n:buildingCount}} buildings. This page lists every one of them with the numbers for every level, in the order you meet them. The tables are generated from the game's own data, so they match what you see in the game.

## How buildings work

- **The Citadel sets the limits.** Its level decides which buildings you may place, how many of each, and how high they can go. A building that unlocks at Citadel 3 is level 1 at Citadel 3, level 2 at Citadel 4, and so on.
- **Every job needs a builder.** You have two builders at the start, three from Citadel 4 and four from Citadel 7. Walls are the exception: they go up at once and need nobody.
- **Oil or scrap.** Oil pays for the Citadel, the economy and the army. Scrap pays for everything that defends: guns, walls, mines and snares.
- **Moving is free.** Tap a building and drag it. You can rearrange the whole base at any time, at no cost.
- **Removing gives half back.** Demolish a building and you get {{n:refund}}% of everything you spent on it. The Citadel cannot be removed.
- **Raids break nothing for good.** After an attack your buildings are all still there. Raiders take oil, scrap and trophies, never buildings.
- **A building that is being upgraded still stands.** In a raid it fights or stores at its current level. A brand-new building that is not finished yet counts as level 1.

The heart of the base. Its level unlocks everything else, it stores oil and scrap, and destroying it is worth a star to the attacker. Keep it inside your walls and inside the range of your guns. [What each Citadel level unlocks](/blog/citadel-upgrade-guide/) has its own page.

{{building:collector}}

Your oil income. A Pumpjack fills up in {{b:collector.1.fullAfter}} at every level and then stops, so empty it often. It produces nothing while it is being upgraded: upgrade Pumpjacks one at a time, not all at once.

{{building:storage}}

Oil Tanks, together with the Citadel, decide how much oil you can hold. That matters twice: a Citadel upgrade has to fit in your stores before you can pay for it, and loot from a raid is lost if there is no room for it.

{{levels:storage}}

{{building:salvage}}

Cuts scrap out of a crashed ship, at the same rates as a Pumpjack pumps oil. Build one in your first minutes: without it, raids are your only source of scrap, and scrap is what guns and walls cost.

{{gallery:salvage}}

{{levels:salvage}}

{{building:depot}}

The scrap twin of the Oil Tank. Late in the game defenses cost far more scrap than the Citadel alone can hold, so keep your depots at the level your Citadel allows.

{{levels:depot}}

{{building:barracks}}

Every Barracks adds army space, and army space is how many troops you can land in one raid. Units themselves are free and need no training. A second, third and fourth Barracks are among the best things a new Citadel level brings.

{{gallery:barracks}}

{{levels:barracks}}

{{building:proving}}

Here you research units to higher levels, one at a time, for oil. A Proving Ground of level 1 lets units reach level 2, level 2 lets them reach level 3, up to unit level 6. Research needs no builder, so keep it running. The [units guide](/blog/dune-clash-units-guide/) lists what every level gives.

{{levels:proving}}

{{building:motor}}

Unlocks vehicles: the Ram at Citadel 3, the Scavenger at 4, the Bulwark at 5, the Longshot and the Wrench Rig at 6. It also adds a little army space of its own.

{{gallery:motor}}

{{levels:motor}}

{{building:rotor}}

Unlocks aircraft: the Flyer at Citadel 4 and the Thunderwing at Citadel 7. Aircraft ignore walls, mines and the worm, which makes this one of the big steps in the game. See the [air guide](/blog/air-units-guide-flyer-thunderwing/).

{{levels:rotor}}

{{building:turret}}

Your first gun and the one you will own most of. Fast, cheap, and at its best against Infantry, Rangers and Scavengers. It cannot shoot aircraft.

{{gallery:turret}}

{{levels:turret}}

{{building:shellpit}}

A mortar. Its shells hit an area, so it punishes troops that walk in a tight group. It cannot hit anything closer than three cells, and it cannot hit aircraft. Place it deep inside the base with other guns covering its blind ring.

{{levels:shellpit}}

{{building:flak}}

The only gun made for aircraft, and useless against anything else. From Citadel 4 on, a base without a Flak Nest is an open door for Flyers. Put it near the middle, where it covers most of the base.

{{levels:flak}}

{{building:blind}}

A hidden gun. Raiders do not see it while they scout; it rises when an enemy comes within 4.5 cells, or once more than half the base is gone. It fires fast, hits ground and air, and does extra damage to armoured vehicles. More in [hidden defenses](/blog/traps-guide-mines-sand-blinds-sky-snares/).

{{levels:blind}}

{{building:rocket}}

The heaviest gun in the game: long reach, ground and air, extra damage to armour. It reloads slowly, so it is strongest next to faster guns that deal with crowds.

{{gallery:rocket}}

{{levels:rocket}}

{{building:wall}}

Walls stop ground units until they are broken or walked around. They are built at once, need no builder, and do not count towards the attacker's destruction percentage. The [walls guide](/blog/walls-guide/) explains how units decide whether to go through or around.

{{gallery:wall}}

{{levels:wall}}

{{building:trap}}

A buried charge that raiders cannot see. It goes off when a ground unit comes within about a cell and damages everything around it. Mines are back in place for the next raid: nothing has to be bought again.

{{levels:trap}}

{{building:snare}}

The Mine's partner against aircraft: a hidden launcher that fires one heavy burst at anything that flies over it. One Sky Snare brings down a Flyer of any level.

{{levels:snare}}

## What to upgrade first

A short rule that works at every Citadel level:

1. **Storage you need for the next big purchase.** If the next Citadel level does not fit in your tanks, nothing else matters.
2. **Income.** Pumpjacks and Salvage Yards pay for themselves.
3. **Army.** Barracks for space, the Proving Ground for stronger units.
4. **Guns**, starting with the newest type you have unlocked.
5. **Walls**, with whatever scrap is left over. They need no builder, so they never hold anything up.

The [beginner's guide](/blog/dune-clash-beginners-guide/) shows this order in practice, and the [defenses guide](/blog/dune-clash-defenses-guide/) goes deeper on the guns.
