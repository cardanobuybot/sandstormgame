Nobody plays a new strategy game well in the first week. These are the ten mistakes we see most often in Dune Clash, roughly in the order people make them. Each one is cheap to fix once you know about it.

## 1. Leaving a builder idle

Everything in the game is limited by builders: two until Citadel 4, three until Citadel 7, four after that. A builder with nothing to do is time you never get back.

**Do this instead:** before you close the game, give every builder a job, and give the longest job to the builder who will be working while you sleep.

## 2. Letting Pumpjacks and Salvage Yards fill up

A Pumpjack or Salvage Yard works around the clock, but only until it is full. A level 1 Pumpjack holds six hours of oil; after that it produces nothing. And when someone raids you, they take half of whatever is sitting inside.

**Do this instead:** tap **Collect all** every time you open the game.

## 3. Forgetting about scrap

New players build Pumpjacks, watch the oil counter grow, then find they cannot afford a single wall. Guns, walls, mines and snares are paid with scrap, not oil.

**Do this instead:** build a Salvage Yard and a Scrap Depot in your first minutes, and keep them at the same level as your Pumpjacks and Oil Tanks. The [economy guide](/blog/oil-and-scrap-economy-guide/) explains the split.

## 4. Raiding with full stores

Loot has to fit. If your Oil Tanks are full, a raid brings home no oil at all: the result screen will say something like "+0 of 1,600". The same goes for scrap and Scrap Depots.

**Do this instead:** spend first, raid second. The oil and scrap counters at the top of your base fill up like gauges, and while you scout the game says so in plain words: "Your tanks are full: this raid will bring home no oil." Believe it.

## 5. Dropping the whole army on one spot

One Shellpit shell or one Mine can wipe out a tight group of Infantry, and the Sandmaw's bite hits everything within {{n:wormSplash}} cells.

**Do this instead:** spread foot soldiers along a stretch of the edge. Hold your finger down and slide it instead of tapping the same place twenty times.

## 6. Ignoring the tremor reading

While you scout, a chip at the top says **Tremors: low** or **Tremors: high**. High means the Sandmaw will come during this battle. Many new players never look at it and then lose a column of tanks to a worm they could have planned for.

**Do this instead:** on "high", bring aircraft, or keep enough troops together to kill the worm when it surfaces. [How to beat the Sandmaw](/blog/how-to-beat-the-sandmaw/) has the details.

## 7. Flying into Flak

Aircraft feel unbeatable the first time: they ignore walls, Turrets, Mines and the worm. Then you meet a base with two Flak Nests and lose six Flyers in ten seconds.

**Do this instead:** count the Flak Nests while you scout. One is manageable. With two or more, send ground troops to break them first, or leave the aircraft at home. Remember that Sky Snares are hidden until an aircraft flies over them.

## 8. Rushing the Citadel with level 1 guns

The Citadel unlocks everything, so it is tempting to upgrade nothing else. But opponents are picked by Citadel level. A Citadel 5 base with Citadel 2 defenses gets raided by Citadel 5 armies, and it loses.

**Do this instead:** before each Citadel upgrade, bring your storage, your Barracks and your main guns to the level the current Citadel allows. You do not need to max every wall.

## 9. Spending crystals on short timers

The game gives you {{n:startCrystals}} crystals and Gus shows you the **Finish now** button early on. It is easy to burn the lot on ten-minute jobs in the first evening.

**Do this instead:** keep crystals for moments that matter: a Citadel upgrade you are a few thousand oil short of, or a long job you want done before a busy week. The [crystals guide](/blog/dune-clash-crystals-guide/) lists what they cost and where free ones come from.

## 10. Not saving the base

This one hurts the most. Your base is stored on our server, but the key to it lives on your device. A broken phone or a cleared browser means a lost base, unless you saved it.

**Do this instead:** open ☰ today. On iPhone tap **Save progress with Game Center**; on Android, **Save progress with Google**. In a browser, use **Show code** to move the base to a phone. It takes a minute: [the full instructions are here](/blog/save-and-transfer-your-base/).

## A bonus mistake: never watching your defense replays

Every raid on your base is recorded. Open **Log**, tap a raid and watch it. You will see which wall the Rams came through, which gun never fired, and where the raiders found your oil. Fix that one thing, and the next raider gets less. The [base layout guide](/blog/dune-clash-base-layout-guide/) shows what to look for.
