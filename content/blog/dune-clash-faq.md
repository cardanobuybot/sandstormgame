## About the game

### What is Dune Clash?

Dune Clash is a free build-and-raid strategy game. You build a base on a desert planet, pump oil, cut crashed spaceships into scrap, and raid other players' bases in three-minute battles while a giant sand worm hunts your ground troops. [Read the full introduction](/blog/what-is-dune-clash/).

### Who makes Dune Clash?

Sandstorm Game, an independent studio run by one developer. It is the studio's first game and came out in November 2026. More on the [about page](/about/).

### Is Dune Clash related to Dune or to Clash of Clans?

No. It is an original game and is not connected with the Dune novels and films or their owners, or with Supercell. The name describes the game: a fight among sand dunes. [The longer answer](/blog/is-dune-clash-related-to-dune-or-clash-of-clans/).

### Is Dune Clash free?

Yes. It is free to download and play and has no adverts. The iPhone and Android apps sell optional packs of crystals, which shorten waiting times. [What crystals cost and do](/blog/dune-clash-crystals-guide/).

### What age is Dune Clash for?

It is rated 9+ on the App Store, and we make it for players of 13 and up. It has cartoon battles, no blood, no chat and no adverts. [A guide for parents](/blog/is-dune-clash-safe-for-kids-parents-guide/).

## Getting the game

### Where can I download Dune Clash?

On iPhone, from the App Store. On any device, you can play in a web browser with nothing to install. The Android version is in closed testing and not yet public. [All the official links](/blog/how-to-download-dune-clash/).

### Is Dune Clash on Android?

Not publicly yet. The Android version is in closed testing on Google Play. Until it opens, Android players can use the browser version, which is the same game. Do not install files offered under our name on other sites: we publish none.

### Why can I not find Dune Clash in the App Store in my country?

The game is not listed in the App Store of European Union countries yet, while Apple finishes checking the developer's trader details as EU law requires. Elsewhere, search for "Dune Clash". EU players can use the browser version in the meantime.

### Can I play Dune Clash on a computer?

Yes, in a web browser, with a mouse. There is no separate program to install. [How the browser version works](/blog/play-dune-clash-in-browser/).

### Does Dune Clash work offline?

No. Your base lives on the game's server and other players raid it, so the game needs an internet connection.

### Which languages does Dune Clash support?

English only, for now.

## Your account

### Do I need an account to play?

No. The game creates a guest account on your device the first time you open it. There is no e-mail address and no password.

### How do I save my progress?

Open the menu ☰ and tap Save progress with Game Center on iPhone, or Save progress with Google on Android. In a browser, move the base to another device with a transfer code. [Step by step](/blog/save-and-transfer-your-base/).

### How do I move my base to a new phone?

If it is saved to Game Center or Google, tap the same button on the new phone and load it. Between iPhone, Android and the browser, use a one-time transfer code: ☰, Show code on the old device, ☰, Enter code on the new one.

### How do I delete my account?

Open the menu ☰, tap Delete account and confirm. The base and all its data are erased from the server at once and cannot be restored.

### How do I change my commander name?

Open the menu ☰, type a new name of up to 16 characters and tap Save.

## Playing

### How long does a raid last?

You get {{n:scoutSeconds}} seconds to scout and then {{n:battleMinutes}} minutes to fight. The battle ends early if you destroy everything or have no troops left to send.

### Do I have to train troops?

No. Units cost nothing and need no training. Before each raid you choose any mix of the units you have unlocked, up to your army space. [All nine units](/blog/dune-clash-units-guide/).

### What is the Sandmaw?

A giant sand worm that appears in about half of all raids and attacks ground troops of the attacking side. It ignores buildings and cannot reach aircraft, and it can be killed while it is above the sand. The scouting screen warns you with "Tremors: high". [How to beat it](/blog/how-to-beat-the-sandmaw/).

### How do I get three stars?

One star is for destroying half the buildings, one for destroying the Citadel, and the third for destroying everything. Walls and traps do not count. [The attack guide](/blog/how-to-get-three-stars-attack-guide/).

### What do oil and scrap do?

Oil pays for the Citadel, the economy, army buildings and research. Scrap pays for defenses, walls, mines and snares. Pumpjacks make oil, Salvage Yards make scrap, and raids bring both. [The economy guide](/blog/oil-and-scrap-economy-guide/).

### What is the maximum Citadel level?

Level {{n:citadelMax}}. Each level unlocks new buildings and units and raises the level cap of the others. [What each level unlocks](/blog/citadel-upgrade-guide/).

### How many builders can I have?

Two at the start, three from Citadel 4 and four from Citadel 7. Builders are not sold.

### Are there clans or chat in Dune Clash?

No, not today. There is no chat of any kind. Clans are the next large feature planned.

## Being raided

### What do I lose when my base is raided?

Some oil, some scrap and some trophies. A raid can take at most 20% of what you have stored (16% from Citadel 5, 14% from Citadel 7) and half of what sits uncollected in your Pumpjacks and Salvage Yards. No building is ever lost. [The loot rules](/blog/loot-guide-how-much-oil-and-scrap-can-you-steal/).

### Do I have to rebuild after an attack?

No. Every building, wall and mine is back at once. There are no repairs.

### How do shields work?

If a raid takes {{n:shieldShare}}% or more of the loot that was on offer, your base is shielded for {{n:shieldHours}} hours, and raids during that time cost you nothing. Attacking others does not remove your shield. [Shields and revenge](/blog/trophies-ladder-shields-explained/).

### What are Echo and Outpost bases?

An Echo is a copy of a real player's base; its owner loses nothing when you raid it. An Outpost is a base built by the computer. You meet them when no suitable real opponent is free, and the scouting screen always says which kind you are facing.

### Can people cheat in Dune Clash?

Results cannot be faked. Your device sends only the list of troops you landed, and the server replays the whole battle and works out the stars and loot itself. [How it works](/blog/fair-play-server-replays-anti-cheat/).

## Money

### What can I buy, and do I have to?

Only crystals, in packs from US $0.99 to US $49.99, in the iPhone and Android apps. They finish timers and top up oil or scrap. You never have to buy any: everything in the game can be earned by playing, and the browser version sells nothing. [The free-to-play guide](/blog/free-to-play-guide/).
