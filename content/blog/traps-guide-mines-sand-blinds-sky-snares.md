Most of your base is an open book: the raider gets {{n:scoutSeconds}} seconds to read it before the first soldier lands. Three defenses are not in that book. Mines, Sky Snares and Sand Blinds stay invisible until they act, and they are the reason a careful attack can still go wrong.

## The three at a glance

| | Mine | Sky Snare | Sand Blind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unlocks at | Citadel 2 | Citadel 5 | Citadel 5 |
| Hits | Ground units | Aircraft | Ground units and aircraft |
| Works | Once per raid | Once per raid | Keeps firing until destroyed |
| Goes off when | A ground unit comes within 1.2 cells | An aircraft comes within 1.6 cells | An enemy comes within 4.5 cells |
| How many at Citadel 8 | {{limit:trap.8}} | {{limit:snare.8}} | {{limit:blind.8}} |
| Paid with | Scrap | Scrap | Scrap |

A raider sees none of them while scouting, and the sand around them looks like any other sand. You, watching the replay of a raid on your own base, see everything.

## Mines

{{pic:b-trap-7|A Mine, shown here above the sand. In a raid it is buried.}}

A Mine waits until a ground unit comes within 1.2 cells, then blows up and damages every ground unit inside its blast. Aircraft never set it off and are never hurt by it.

{{levels:trap}}

What the numbers mean in practice:

- A level 1 Mine does 110 damage. Infantry start with 70 health and Rangers with 55, so **one Mine wipes out a whole cluster of foot soldiers**.
- Against a Bulwark with 1,500 health, a Mine is a scratch. Mines are for crowds, not for tanks.
- The blast grows a little with each level, from 2 cells to 2.6.

**Where to put them:**

1. **In the gaps.** Any opening in your walls is where Infantry will walk. Put a Mine in it.
2. **In front of the loot.** Raiders drop troops on the side nearest your Oil Tanks and Scrap Depots. The sand between the edge and the tanks is the busiest road in your base.
3. **Not right at the edge.** Troops land on the strip outside the base and bunch up when they reach a wall or a building. A Mine a few cells in catches the bunch.
4. **Apart from each other.** Two Mines side by side go off together and kill the same soldiers twice.

A Mine that fires is back in place for the next raid. You never buy it again.

## Sky Snares

A Sky Snare is a Mine for aircraft: a hidden launcher that fires one burst at anything flying within 1.6 cells, and hits every aircraft within 2.4 cells.

{{levels:snare}}

A level 1 Sky Snare does 320 damage. A Flyer has between 130 and 229 health depending on its level, so **one Sky Snare always kills a Flyer**, and usually the two or three flying next to it. A Thunderwing survives one snare, badly hurt, and dies to the third.

**Where to put them:**

- **On the way to your defenses.** Flyers and Thunderwings go for guns first. Draw a line from the edge of the map to your nearest gun: that is their flight path.
- **Near the Flak Nest.** Raiders like to kill a Flak Nest with ground troops and then send aircraft through the hole. A snare beside the Flak Nest is waiting for exactly that.
- **Spread out.** Each snare fires once. Two in the same spot waste the second.

## Sand Blinds

{{pic:blind-hidden|A Sand Blind before it rises: nothing to see.}}

The Sand Blind is not a trap but a real gun that starts the battle hidden. It rises when an enemy it can hit comes within 4.5 cells and opens fire a moment later. From then on it is an ordinary building: it can be shot, and it counts towards the destruction percentage.

{{levels:blind}}

It fires twice a second, hits ground and air, and deals ×1.5 damage to armoured vehicles: Rams, Bulwarks, Longshots and Wrench Rigs. Its reach is 5.5 cells, the shortest of any gun, so it has to be close to the action.

Two more ways a Sand Blind gets revealed:

- **When {{n:revealPercent}}% of the base is destroyed**, every hidden gun rises, wherever the enemy is. Nobody loses a three-star raid to a building they could not find.
- **When it is hit.** A Thunderwing's bombs damage everything under them, including a Sand Blind nobody has seen yet.

**Where to put them:**

- **Behind the wall a Ram will break.** The breach is where the tanks come through, and tanks are what this gun is for.
- **Beside something tempting**: an Oil Tank near the edge, a lone Pumpjack. Whatever looks like easy loot.
- **Not next to your other guns.** Raiders drop troops near visible guns on purpose. A hidden gun is worth most where the raider thought it was safe.

## How raiders deal with hidden defenses

Know the counter-play, because you will be on the other side too.

- **A scout unit.** One Infantry soldier sent ahead costs one army space and sets off a Mine or wakes a Sand Blind before the real army arrives.
- **Spreading out.** A line of troops loses three soldiers to a Mine; a clump loses twenty.
- **Reading the empty spaces.** A Sand Blind needs a free square two cells wide. An odd empty patch between buildings may have one under it. From Citadel 5 on, assume there is a Sand Blind you cannot see; from Citadel 7, two.
- **Aircraft against Mines, ground troops against snares.** Each trap works on one kind of unit only.

The [scouting guide](/blog/scouting-guide-30-seconds/) turns this into a thirty-second routine.

## Upgrade order

Mines are cheap at first and worth keeping level with the Citadel until about level 4. After that each level costs as much as a gun level and adds less. In the late game put scrap into the Sand Blind first, then Sky Snares if you are being raided from the air, then Mines.
