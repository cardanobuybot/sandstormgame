A raid in Dune Clash lasts {{n:battleMinutes}} minutes, and you control only two things: which troops you bring and where you put them down. After that they think for themselves. This guide is about making those two decisions well.

## How stars are counted

{{table:trophies}}

Three details matter more than they seem to:

- **Destruction is counted in buildings, not in size.** A Pumpjack counts exactly as much as the Citadel. Twenty buildings, ten destroyed: 50%.
- **Walls, Mines and Sky Snares do not count.** Breaking walls earns nothing by itself.
- **The Citadel is a star of its own**, whatever the percentage.

So there are two cheap stars and one expensive one. Half the buildings can be had from the outside of most bases. The Citadel takes a push to the middle. The third star needs every last building, including the one in the far corner.

## The clock

- **Scouting: {{n:scoutSeconds}} seconds.** The base is frozen and you can look around. Landing your first unit ends scouting and starts the battle.
- **Battle: {{n:battleSeconds}} seconds.** It ends early when everything is destroyed, or when you have no fighter alive and no army space to land another.
- **Leaving before you land anything costs nothing.** The raid simply never happened. **Next** finds another opponent for a little oil.
- **End battle** stops the fight and keeps the stars and loot you have already earned.

Your troops are free. Losing all of them costs nothing but the raid.

## Know what your troops will do

You cannot steer units after they land, so learn where they go by themselves.

| They go for | Units |
| --- | --- |
| The nearest building, whatever it is | Infantry, Ranger, Ram |
| Oil and scrap buildings first | Scavenger |
| Defenses first | Flyer, Bulwark, Longshot, Thunderwing |
| Your own vehicles, to repair them | Wrench Rig |

"Nearest" is measured from where the unit stands, so **the landing spot is the steering wheel**. Infantry landed next to a Barracks will attack the Barracks. Infantry landed next to a Turret will attack the Turret, and be shot by it.

All nine units, with numbers, are in the [units guide](/blog/dune-clash-units-guide/).

## A plan in five steps

### 1. Decide what you came for

Before the first tap, pick one:

- **Loot.** Find the Oil Tanks, Scrap Depots and the Citadel: that is where the stored loot is. Pumpjacks and Salvage Yards hold the rest.
- **One or two stars, fast.** Half the base, or the Citadel.
- **Three stars.** Everything, which means a plan for the far side too.

### 2. Choose a side

Look for the side where the most valuable thing is closest to the edge and the fewest guns can reach it. Guns shoot the nearest enemy inside their circle, so a corner covered by one Turret is a very different place from a corner covered by three.

### 3. Land the shield first

Something tough goes in before anything fragile: a Bulwark, a pair of Rams, or at low levels simply the first ten Infantry. Guns lock on to whatever is nearest. Give them something that can take it.

### 4. Land the damage behind it

Two or three seconds later, the units that do the work: Rangers behind the tanks, Longshots well back, Infantry in a line. Hold your finger down and **drag along the edge** to land a line; tapping one spot lands a clump, and clumps die to Shellpits, Mines and the Sandmaw.

### 5. Keep a reserve

Do not land everything in the first ten seconds. Keep about a fifth of your army space for the last minute. You will need it for one of three things: a second wave when the first one stalls, bait for the worm, or the clean-up described next.

## The corner building problem

Most failed three-star raids end at 96% with twenty seconds left and one Pumpjack on the far side of the map. Your army is alive and walking towards it. It will not get there.

The base is {{n:grid}} cells across. A Bulwark covers 1.1 cells a second: more than half a minute to cross it. Infantry manage 2 cells a second, Rangers 1.8.

The fix is cheap: **land a few units next to the leftover buildings.** You can deploy anywhere on the strip around the base at any time. Three Infantry dropped beside a lonely Pumpjack finish it while the main army is still busy in the middle. While you scout, note which buildings stand apart, and save a handful of army space for each.

## Getting through walls

Infantry, Scavengers and Rangers prefer to walk around a wall if a gap is not too far, and that walk is usually past guns. From Citadel 3, bring **Rams**: they deal twelve times their damage to walls and drive straight at them. Land the Rams a moment before the rest, at the point where you want the hole.

Aircraft skip the question. The [walls guide](/blog/walls-guide/) explains exactly how each unit chooses.

## Reading the guns

- **Turrets** hurt Infantry, Rangers and Scavengers most. Lead with armour.
- **Shellpits** cannot hit anything within three cells of themselves. Units that get close are safe from that one.
- **Flak Nests** shoot only aircraft. Kill them with ground troops, then fly.
- **Rocket Batteries and Sand Blinds** deal extra damage to vehicles. Swarm them with cheap infantry.
- **From Citadel 5**, expect a gun you cannot see.

Every gun is described in the [defenses guide](/blog/dune-clash-defenses-guide/).

## Mind the worm

If the scouting screen says **Tremors: high**, the Sandmaw will arrive between {{n:wormFrom}} and {{n:wormTo}} seconds in and go for your ground troops. Spread out, keep a reserve, or fly. The full playbook is in [how to beat the Sandmaw](/blog/how-to-beat-the-sandmaw/).

## Five habits of good raiders

1. **Use all thirty seconds.** The base will not change, and the clock for the battle has not started.
2. **Tap buildings while scouting.** The panel shows level, health and, for guns, range and what they can hit.
3. **Skip bases that do not suit your army.** A few oil for **Next** is cheaper than a lost raid's worth of time.
4. **Watch your own replays.** The **Log** keeps your raids. Watching where the army went wrong is the fastest way to stop repeating it.
5. **Practise on Echoes and Outposts.** Nobody loses anything in those raids, and you still earn loot, stars and trophies.
