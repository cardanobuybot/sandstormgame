Every raid in Dune Clash has two sides. Half of them have a third: the Sandmaw, a giant sand worm that belongs to nobody and eats whatever walks on the sand. It can ruin a perfect attack in ten seconds. It can also be dodged, outlasted or killed, once you know its rules.

{{shot:worm|The Sandmaw surfaces under a group of troops.}}

## The rules of the worm

- **It comes in {{n:wormChance}}% of raids.** Whether it will is decided before the battle starts, and you are told: while you scout, a chip at the top reads **Tremors: low** or **Tremors: high**.
- **It arrives between {{n:wormFrom}} and {{n:wormTo}} seconds** into the battle, from a random edge of the map.
- **It hunts ground units only**: soldiers and vehicles. It always goes for the nearest one.
- **It ignores buildings and cannot reach aircraft.** It does no harm to the base, and the base's guns cannot see it.
- **It travels under the sand in a straight line**, under walls and buildings, at {{n:wormSpeed}} cells a second. That is faster than every ground unit except the Scavenger.
- **It bursts out under its prey and bites**: {{n:wormDamage}} damage to every ground unit within {{n:wormSplash}} cells, every {{n:wormReload}} seconds.
- **After a bite it stays above the sand** for {{n:wormSurface}} seconds, and every new bite keeps it up longer. With nothing left to bite, it dives and goes looking again.
- **It leaves after {{n:wormStay}} seconds**, whatever happens.
- **Above the sand it can be killed.**

## How tough is it?

The worm's health grows with your army: {{n:wormBase}} plus {{n:wormPer}} for every point of army space you own. It counts the army you *could* field, not the troops you have landed so far.

A bite kills low-level Infantry and Rangers outright, and it hits everything in a circle more than three cells across. A tight group of twenty soldiers can be gone in one bite. A Bulwark, on the other hand, shrugs off a dozen.

## Your troops fight back

When the worm is above the sand, every fighter within {{n:wormAlert}} cells drops what it is doing and attacks it. Ground troops walk around buildings to reach it; aircraft fly straight there, and the worm cannot touch them. When it dives or dies, everyone goes back to the base.

So the fight is automatic. What you control is *who is standing near the worm when it comes up*.

## Three ways to deal with it

### 1. Fly

An army with nothing on the ground gives the worm no prey. It never surfaces, and after {{n:wormStay}} seconds it is gone. From Citadel 4, an all-air raid on a "high tremors" base makes the worm irrelevant, as long as the base's anti-air does not. See the [air guide](/blog/air-units-guide-flyer-thunderwing/).

Even a few Flyers in a ground army help: they join the fight when the worm surfaces, and it cannot bite back.

### 2. Kill it

The worm is dangerous to a few units and helpless against many. Look at Citadel 3: it has 894 health. Thirty level 1 Infantry deal 360 damage a second between them. If most of them are close enough to reach it, the worm lasts about three seconds above the sand.

To make that happen:

- **Keep your army in one or two loose groups**, not scattered in ones and twos around the map. A lone unit gets eaten; a group kills the worm.
- **Loose, not tight.** The bite reaches {{n:wormSplash}} cells. Hold your finger down and drag along the edge when you land troops, so they arrive in a line.
- **Put something heavy in front of the soft units.** The worm goes for the nearest ground unit. If that is a Bulwark with 1,500 health, Rangers standing a few cells behind it get eighteen bites' worth of free shooting.

### 3. Outwait it

The worm is on the map for {{n:wormStay}} seconds, and a raid lasts {{n:battleSeconds}} seconds. You do not have to land everything at once.

- **Hold back half the army.** Land a first wave, let the worm arrive and spend its time on it, and land the second wave when it dives or leaves.
- **Use bait.** One Infantry soldier dropped in a far corner pulls the worm there, if it is the nearest ground unit at that moment. At {{n:wormSpeed}} cells a second the worm needs more than twenty seconds to cross the map, which is nearly half its stay.
- **Wait for it before committing vehicles.** If the tremors are high and nothing has surfaced after a minute and a half, it is about to.

## What not to do

- **Do not ignore the chip.** "Tremors: high" is free information. The same army and the same landing spot are right for a quiet raid and wrong for a worm raid.
- **Do not drop a column of Rams or Longshots alone.** They are slow, they cannot fight back well, and the worm is faster than they are.
- **Do not send Wrench Rigs without fighters.** Repair trucks cannot attack. The worm treats them as food like anything else on wheels.
- **Do not panic and land everything on top of it.** Reinforcements dropped next to a surfaced worm arrive inside its bite.

## The worm is on the defender's side, sort of

When your own base is raided, the Sandmaw is a free defense that costs no scrap. You cannot summon it or steer it, but you can help it: bases that force ground troops to take a long walk around walls give the worm more time to find them.

## Common questions

**Does killing the worm give a reward?** No loot and no stars. The reward is the army you keep.

**Can it show up twice in one raid?** No. One worm, one visit.

**Does it attack in replays the same way?** Yes. A replay is the same battle played again, worm included.

**Where does it come from?** From the sand, and from a long line of [giant worms in fiction and games](/blog/sand-worms-in-games-and-fiction/). Ours is its own beast, with its own rules.
