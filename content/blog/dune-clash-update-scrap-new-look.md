Dune Clash came out on the App Store on 24 November 2026. One day later, version 1.0.1 is out, and it is a bigger update than the number suggests. Here is what changed.

## A second resource: scrap

Until now the game ran on oil alone. Version 1.0.1 adds **scrap**, and with it two buildings:

- the **Salvage Yard**, which cuts scrap out of a crashed spaceship around the clock;
- the **Scrap Depot**, which stores it.

{{gallery:salvage}}

The planet in Dune Clash is littered with ships that fell on it. A Salvage Yard is a crew taking one of them apart.

**What scrap pays for:** Turrets, Shellpits, Flak Nests, Sand Blinds, Rocket Batteries, walls, Mines and Sky Snares. Everything that defends your base is now built from scrap. Oil keeps the Citadel, the economy, the army buildings and research.

**Why:** with one resource, every choice was the same choice. A player could pour all their oil into Pumpjacks and the Citadel and leave the guns for later, or the reverse. With two, your defenses have a budget of their own, and raiding has a second thing worth going after.

**What it means for raids:** you now carry off oil *and* scrap, each counted separately at the top of the battle screen, and both have to fit in your stores.

**If you had a base before the update:** nothing you built has changed, and you received a one-time stock of scrap to keep upgrading your defenses: at least {{n:startScrap}}, and more at higher Citadel levels. Build a Salvage Yard and a Scrap Depot first; both are available from Citadel 1.

The whole system is explained in [the economy guide](/blog/oil-and-scrap-economy-guide/).

## A new look

The interface has been redrawn:

- **Icons** on the buttons and counters.
- **A closer view of your base.**
- **Builders who walk to their work.** When you start an upgrade, a builder walks over to the building and hammers away until it is done. The free ones stroll around the base.
- **Two counters at the top**, one for oil and one for scrap, that fill up like gauges as your stores do.

{{shots:new-base,upgrade|A new base in the new interface.|The upgrade panel: what the next level gives, in green.}}

## Livelier raids

Battles follow exactly the same rules as before. They just show more of what is going on:

- buildings **burn** as they take damage and collapse when they fall;
- **loot flies** out of a building to your counter when you destroy it;
- soldiers **aim** before they fire, and fall when they are hit.

{{shot:deploy|Troops going in from the edge of the map.}}

## New music

Your base has a new home theme, "Sandmaw Rising". If you would rather play in silence, or with your own music, the menu ☰ now has separate **volume sliders for sound and music**.

## How to get the update

- **iPhone:** version 1.0.1 is on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/dune-clash/id6837478004). If your phone updates apps automatically, you may already have it.
- **Browser:** nothing to do. The [browser version](/blog/play-dune-clash-in-browser/) is always the latest.
- **Android:** the same changes are in the build now in closed testing. The public release comes later.
